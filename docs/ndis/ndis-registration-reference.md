# NDIS Registration Reference

Working reference for Care Together Services' NDIS provider registration. Use it to pick the registration groups (supports and services) we will deliver, then plan the audit each group requires.

## Audit rules

- **Verification audit:** for low-risk, low-complexity supports. Desktop review of documentary evidence by an Approved Quality Auditor.
- **Certification audit:** for higher-risk or more complex supports. Two stages: a desktop audit (Stage 1) and an onsite audit (Stage 2) within 3 months of Stage 1.
- **Mixed application:** if the application includes groups needing both types, a certification audit is required.
- **Mid-term audit:** required 12 months after registration for providers that completed a certification audit. Must be finalised by 18 months.
- **Condition audit:** can be required by the NDIS Commission during the registration period.
- **Out of cycle audit:** needed to change supports or services during the registration period.
- **Audit ratings:** 3 (best practice), 2 (conforms), 1 (minor non-conformity), 0 (major non-conformity, 3 months to fix).
- **Audit report deadlines:** 14 days after a verification audit, 28 days after a certification or mid-term audit.
- **Participant notice:** participants are automatically included in the audit (opt-out). Notify them, record any opt-out, and tell the auditor.
- **Costs:** there is no cost to register with the NDIS Commission. Auditor fees vary, so get multiple quotes.

## Quality audit process

1. Engage an Approved Quality Auditor. Compare quotes and confirm the scope and timing.
2. Review the NDIS Practice Standards that apply to our selected groups.
3. Gather documents and evidence, including qualifications and worker screening clearances.
4. Tell staff and participants that auditors will visit and observe practice.
5. Respond promptly to any major or minor non-conformities.
6. Confirm the auditor submits the report to the NDIS Commission by the deadline.
